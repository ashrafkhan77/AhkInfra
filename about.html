Welcome to AHK Infra

We are one of the leading Structural steel detailing organization with ISO-9001:2015 certification. Our services include steel detailing,structural detailing, structural design, 3D structural Model. We provide very high quality of structure detailing services at a very competitive rates and we always deliver on time.

We have a team of talented engineers who have in-depth knowledge of steel detailing and are always ready try new things in steel structure detailing. We work on both day and night shifts so that our clients get responded ASAP.

Our structural engineering services include:

  • Structural detailing
  • Structural design, 3D steel structural design services
  • Structural drawings, CAD drawings
  • Structural steel detailing, TEKLA steel detailing
  • Structural analysis design, reinforced concrete structures
  • Structural building 3D models
  • Pre-stressed structure drawings
  • 2D shop drawings and cabinet shop drawings
  • Steel fabrication drawings, erection drawings
